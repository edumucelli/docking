# Docking

Docking is a desktop dock for launching applications, managing windows, and
using applets. Its tours show those interactions on a desktop.

## Language

### Tours

**Tour**

A recorded walkthrough of Docking on a desktop.

**Overview tour**

A short introduction to Docking's main interactions, including application
menus, window previews, folder stacks, and selected applets.

**Applet tour**

A tour that shows built-in applets and their menus in chapters that follow
the applet catalog categories.

**Preferences tour**

A tour that visits every tab in Docking Preferences and ends with a look at
Diagnostics.

**Tour scene**

A segment of a tour that shows a specific Docking interaction.

**Context bar**

A text overlay inside the recorded desktop, above the dock shelf, that gives
the current scene a title and a short explanation.

### Dock interactions

**Window preview**

A popup that shows thumbnails for an application's open windows. Clicking a
thumbnail brings its window forward.

**Folder stack**

A popup that shows the contents of a folder pinned to the dock.
