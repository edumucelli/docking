# Code of Conduct

Everyone should be able to participate in Docking without harassment or
discrimination. This applies in project spaces such as issues, pull requests,
and discussions, and whenever someone officially represents the project.

## Community standards

Treat people with respect, welcome contributors of all backgrounds and
experience levels, and give constructive feedback about the work. Take
responsibility for mistakes and give others room to correct theirs.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory remarks, unwanted sexual attention,
personal attacks, and sharing someone's private information without consent
are unacceptable.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the [Docking maintainer](mailto:edumucelli@gmail.com).
Include what happened and any relevant links or context. The maintainer will
handle reports discreetly and share details only as needed to respond.

The maintainer may remove content, warn participants, or restrict participation
temporarily or permanently. The response will reflect the severity and history
of the behavior.

This policy is adapted from [Contributor Covenant 2.0](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/0/code_of_conduct/).
